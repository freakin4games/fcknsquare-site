# Privacy Policy — Fckn Square

*Last updated: 2026-05-26*

Fckn Square is a breathing app. It is built to get out of your way, and that
includes your data. This policy explains exactly what the app does and does not
collect. No lawyer-speak padding.

## The short version

- **No account.** You never sign in. We never ask who you are.
- **No personal data.** No name, email, contacts, location, health data, or
  anything that identifies you.
- **No breathing data leaves your phone.** Your sessions, counts, and statistics
  are stored only on your device.
- **Anonymous usage stats only.** The app sends a small amount of anonymous,
  aggregate usage data so we can see which features people actually use. Details
  below.
- **No ads. No tracking. No selling data.** Ever.

## What we collect (anonymous usage analytics)

To understand which features get used — so we build the right things — the app
sends anonymous usage events through **TelemetryDeck**, a privacy-focused
analytics service. These events contain only:

- Which breathing pattern, theme, and tracer shape is selected when a session starts
- Which screens are opened (Settings, Statistics)
- When a custom theme is created

That's it. These events carry **no personal information**, no breathing content,
and nothing that could identify you.

TelemetryDeck attaches a **non-reversible, per-install identifier** (a salted
hash) to group events from the same install. This identifier:

- Is **not linked** to your identity
- Is **not** used to track you across other apps or websites
- Uses **no advertising identifier (IDFA)** and triggers **no tracking prompt**

In App Store terms, this is declared as **"Usage Data" (Product Interaction) and
an anonymous Identifier (Device ID)**, both *not linked to you* and *not used for
tracking*.

You can read TelemetryDeck's own privacy approach at
<https://telemetrydeck.com/privacy/>.

## What we never collect

- No name, email address, phone number, or contacts
- No location
- No health or fitness records
- No breathing-session content, notes, or feelings
- No advertising identifiers
- No cross-app or cross-site tracking

## What stays only on your device

- Your breathing statistics (sessions, durations, breath counts)
- Your selected pattern, theme, and tracer shape
- Any custom themes you build

These live in local on-device storage and are never uploaded. Deleting the app
deletes them.

## Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple via the App Store. We never see
your payment details. We only receive, from Apple, whether a product is owned so
the app can unlock the right features.

## Children

Fckn Square is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above changes with it.
