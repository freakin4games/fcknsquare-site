# Fckn Square — Support

A breathing app that does one thing: hold the square, breathe with the haptics,
put it away. If something's not working, here's help.

## Quick start

1. Open the app.
2. Touch and hold the square.
3. Breathe with it — it grows as you inhale, holds, shrinks as you exhale. Your
   phone buzzes the rhythm.
4. Lift your finger to stop. That's the whole thing. No timer, no score.

## FAQ

**I don't feel any buzzing / haptics.**
Haptics need an iPhone with a Taptic Engine (iPhone 8 or newer). Check
Settings → Sounds & Haptics → System Haptics is on. iPads and older devices fall
back to visual-only.

**Why is it called that?**
Because it's not a wellness brand. It's a tool.

**How do I unlock the extra themes / patterns / shapes / stats?**
Settings (gear icon, shown when you're not mid-session) → "Unlock everything"
($8.99, one time, yours forever) — or buy a single theme for $0.99. No subscription.

**I paid but my unlocks aren't showing / I got a new phone.**
Settings → **Restore Purchases**. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

**Do you track me or sell my data?**
No. No account, no ads, no personal data. The app sends only anonymous usage
stats (which features get used). Full detail in the [Privacy Policy](privacy).

**How do I delete my data?**
Settings → "Clear statistics data" wipes your local stats and breath count.
Deleting the app removes everything — nothing is stored off your device.

**Does it work on iPad / Apple Watch?**
iPhone is the main experience (the haptics are the point). iPad runs visual-only.
No Watch app yet.
